# English Translation Style Guide

- Audience: options traders who read American financial English.
- Voice: candid first person, restrained, practical, and reflective. Do not turn the book into a textbook or marketing copy.
- Fidelity: preserve all dates, levels, percentages, formulas, citations, headings, tables, figure order, and risk qualifications.
- Mechanics: positive-gamma hedging is counter-trend; negative-gamma hedging is pro-cyclical. Never reverse these descriptions.
- Terminology: follow `glossary.md`; retain standard Greek and options vocabulary.
- Titles: use concise title case. Keep chapter numbering aligned with Chinese.
- Poems: render Chinese ci as restrained free verse. Preserve stanza and line breaks; do not claim a formal English meter.
- Punctuation: use natural American English punctuation while preserving mathematical symbols and code.
- Images: translate HTML alt text and captions, but never change image paths.
- Links: preserve every source URL and citation number.
- Risk language: translate conservatively; do not strengthen performance claims or weaken disclaimers.
